WhatsApp AI
Intake System.

A short, controlled intake experience for a real professional-services workflow — designed to collect structured information, validate it, store it reliably and escalate to a human when needed.

Product goal

Keep the client-side interaction short — roughly 5 to 7 interactions — while making the workflow behind it robust. Most responses are structured buttons or fields, with very limited free text.

Short flowStructured intakeHuman handoff

Architecture

Python/FastAPI service, PostgreSQL from day one, environment-based configuration, separated storage layer and a Docker-friendly structure designed to migrate to Google Cloud without rewriting the product.

FastAPIPostgreSQLDocker-readyCloud-ready

AI role

The AI layer is used where it adds value: extracting structured information from limited natural-language inputs, checking completeness and producing a clean case draft. Deterministic business rules remain outside the model.

Structured outputsFunction callingValidation

Reliability & safety

The system is being designed around validation, fallbacks, retries, explicit conversation state and a safe human handoff rather than allowing an unconstrained chatbot to improvise.

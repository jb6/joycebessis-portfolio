Shami —
WhatsApp AI Intake System

A production-oriented AI intake assistant for vehicle appraisal and accident cases. It captures structured and unstructured input, validates required information, uses AI to interpret free text, and prepares cases for human review.

My role

Product design, conversation flow, AI workflow design and production-oriented system architecture.

Tech stack

FastAPI, PostgreSQL, WhatsApp Business API, validation layer, LLM structured enrichment and private media storage.

AI capabilities

Free-text understanding, structured case summaries and missing-data support. Optional future extension: multimodal photo analysis.

Human review stays central. AI structures and enriches the case; professional judgment and final review remain with the human team.
